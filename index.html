What's New in M13
☀
Day & Night
A full light mode — Auto, Sun or Moon. Prep at a kitchen table in daylight, switch to dark for the booth. Ambient gets its own Sun identity, and every colour in the app was re-measured so nothing goes faint on a light background.
🎧
My Collection
Your personal music library, sitting alongside your DJ library but never mixed into it. Browse by artist or album, keep your years of ratings and play counts, and pull anything across into the DJ library when it earns its place.
◉
Harmonic set building
The Set Builder now marks every transition in your set — same key, relative, one step round the wheel, or a clash. Arrange reorders the whole set harmonically in a click. Where a clash can be fixed, Fix finds the track that bridges it — and says so honestly when nothing in your library can.
⌘
Select many, act once
Click to select, shift for a range, ⌘A for everything on screen. Then one right-click adds them all to a set, tags them as Bangers, converts them to 432, or fetches their artwork. One action instead of thirty.
⭐
Ratings, Loved and Bangers
Rate out of five on gold vinyl, mark the ones you love, and tag the timeless ones as Bangers — three separate axes, not one. Rate straight from the player bar, so a great track never slips past unmarked while you are listening.
🖼
Brand Artwork
Stamp your own logo onto tracks with no cover, or replace a pool’s branding image. M13 spots pool artwork by finding the same image across different albums — you tick what gets replaced, it never decides for you.

Connect your music collection

M13 reads your Mac's music-library file to show your local collection — it never touches your audio files, and it stays completely separate from your DJ library. To switch it on:

  1. Open the Music app
  2. Menu bar → Music → Settings… → General tab
  3. Tick Share Music Library XML with other applications
  4. Come back here and press Try again
