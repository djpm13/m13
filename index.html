What's New in M13
🎯
432hz Suite — the industry first
M13 scans your library and flags every track tuned to A=432hz. Play anything shifted between 432 and 440 in real time — the pitch moves, the tempo doesn't, so your beatmatching stays solid. Convert tracks permanently to new files whenever you want: your originals are never touched, and BPM, key and artwork all carry over.
🗄
Library Locations
Your library is no longer a single folder. Add as many locations as you like — internal drive, external DJ drive, network share — and add, remove or rescan each one on its own.
📦
Crates
Save named collections of tracks and pull them back up any time. Group the tracks now, decide the running order later.
💾
Sessions
Save your working state and restore it exactly as you left it. Close M13 mid-prep and pick the same session back up next time.
🔭
Scope & Custom Columns
Narrow the whole browse view to just the folders you're working from, and show, hide or reorder the track list columns so the library shows the fields you actually read.
